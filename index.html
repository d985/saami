faq

If you experience challenges with speech, language, voice, or swallowing, you may benefit from speech therapy. Speech therapy addresses several conditions including stroke, brain injury, degenerative diseases (Parkinsons, dementia), or developmental delays.

Speech-Language Pathologists support individuals across all stages of life, whether that means parent coaching to provide an 18-month old with a language-rich environment, rebuilding cognitive-linguistic skills after a stroke or brain injury, or helping someone with Parkinson's regain their voice. Therapy provides the targeted tools needed to navigate the world with clarity and confidence.
